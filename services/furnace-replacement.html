Common Signs You Need Furnace Replacement Services in San Diego, CA

Catching climate control problems early saves money and prevents complete system failure. Contact our team immediately if you notice:

  • Unusual Noises: Grinding, squeaking, or rattling from your indoor fan coil or outdoor compressor unit.
  • Spike in Energy Bills: Sudden jumps in electric or natural gas consumption without an increase in usage.
  • Inconsistent Temperatures: Hot or cold spots in upstairs bedrooms or corner offices.
  • Frequent Short-Cycling: Your system turns on and off rapidly every few minutes.
  • Strange Odors: Musty mildew smells indicating drain line bacteria or burning dust odors.
