About San Diego HVAC Experts

San Diego's Premier Licensed HVAC Contractor Dedicated to High-Efficiency Comfort

Who We Are

San Diego HVAC Experts was founded over 15 years ago with a simple mission: to provide honest, dependable, and technically superior heating and cooling services to residential and commercial property owners across San Diego County.

Living in Southern California brings unique climate challenges. While coastal areas like La Jolla, Point Loma, and Carlsbad experience marine layer moisture and salt air corrosion, inland communities like El Cajon, Poway, and La Mesa face scorching summer heatwaves. Our custom-engineered HVAC systems are designed specifically to perform under these exact conditions.

Our Core Values

  • Technical Integrity: We never guess. Our certified technicians utilize digital gauges, static pressure testing, and airflow calculations to identify root causes.
  • Upfront Flat-Rate Pricing: We believe in complete financial transparency. You will receive a written estimate before any work begins, with zero hidden fees or surprises.
  • Energy Efficiency & Electrification: We are proud pioneers in heat pump technology, dual-fuel hybrid systems, and California Title 24 compliance, helping homeowners lower their carbon footprint and reduce monthly utility expenses.
  • Safety & Licensing: We maintain active California CSLB licensing (#1048291), $2,000,000 general liability insurance, and comprehensive worker's compensation.

Our Quality Guarantees

Every installation and repair performed by San Diego HVAC Experts comes with our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee and a full 1-year labor warranty. If your equipment encounters issues within twelve months of our service, we return and fix it free of charge.
